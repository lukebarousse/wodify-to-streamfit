Wodify → StreamFit Migration Helper

Turn your Wodify performance-history export into files StreamFit's importer accepts — with dates fixed, workouts preserved, and movement names matched to StreamFit's catalog.

Step 1 · Your Wodify file
Drop your Wodify export here or click to choose the file — it looks like UserId1234567_PerformanceResults_*.xlsx
(the file Wodify support emailed you; .xlsx or .csv both work)

🔒 Everything runs in your browser. Your data is never uploaded anywhere.

Step 2 · Match your movements

Match your movements to StreamFit's catalog

Most of your movement names matched StreamFit's catalog exactly. The rest are below — pick the best match for each; we've pre-selected our best guess.

You're only choosing the movement name here. The reps and weight of every individual result import automatically — 1 x 3 @ 315 lbs becomes a 3-rep max at 315 lbs, 1 x 1 @ 325 lbs a 1-rep max at 325. Nothing to type.
Step 3 · Check your data

Review what will be imported

This is your history exactly as it will go into StreamFit. Click ✕ on any row you'd rather leave out — everything else is ready.

Step 4 · Your import files

Ready to import

✓ Dates are written in the US format StreamFit's importer requires — uploading the raw Wodify file directly can silently re-date your whole history to today.
Step 5 · Import into StreamFit
  1. Sign in at go.streamfit.com on a computer.
  2. Go to your profile → PRs tab → Import → tool: Wodify → upload performance_results_prs.csv → Preview Results → check it → Confirm.
  3. Go to the Benchmarks tab → Import → tool: Wodify → upload performance_results_time_exercises.csv → Preview Results → check both the Benchmarks and Workouts tabs of the preview → Confirm.
  4. In the preview, spot-check that dates show your real workout dates (not today's date). If anything looks off, don't confirm — nothing is saved until you do.
